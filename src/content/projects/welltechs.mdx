Welltechs was an industrial website focused on gas and compressor equipment,
EPCM projects, and engineering services. The content is technical, so the
frontend needed to make categories, capabilities, and project context readable
without over-designing the page.

The result is a practical B2B presentation: clear sections, restrained visuals,
and enough structure for visitors to understand the company profile before
moving to contact.
