GoldenKey was a travel-oriented web platform around virtual access to airport
and train station lounges. The product needed to explain the offer quickly,
handle destination-driven browsing, and guide users toward a booking flow.

The frontend work centered on clean content hierarchy, responsive layouts, and
interfaces that make a global catalogue feel manageable. For a travel product,
the UI has to feel trustworthy and lightweight: people need to understand what
they get, where it works, and how to act without reading a manual.
