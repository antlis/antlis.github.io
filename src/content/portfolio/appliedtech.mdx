Appliedtech was a corporate website for an IT services company. The main job
was to present software development, modernization, and workflow optimization
services without turning the page into a generic agency template.

I worked on the frontend structure, responsive layout, and page sections that
explain the company offer in a way that is easy to scan: service blocks,
industry context, trust signals, and contact paths. The goal was a restrained
business site that feels credible and gets out of the way.
