Elmerck is an older business website that is no longer live, but the archived
version still shows the shape of the work: a compact company presence with
clear service pages and a simple content structure.

The project was mostly about turning scattered company information into a site
that could be navigated quickly. It is not a modern showcase anymore, but it is
useful context for earlier commercial frontend work and the kind of pragmatic
sites I shipped before this portfolio was rebuilt.
