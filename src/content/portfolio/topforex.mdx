Topforex was a content-heavy comparison platform for Forex and CFD brokers.
The frontend had to support reviews, comparison-oriented pages, educational
content, and search-driven landing pages without making the interface feel
like a pile of unrelated articles.

The important part was information architecture: reusable layouts for broker
profiles, readable long-form content, comparison blocks, and internal linking
patterns. It is the kind of site where small consistency decisions matter
because users arrive from search with a very specific question.
